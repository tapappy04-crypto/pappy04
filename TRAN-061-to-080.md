## TRAN-061 — Shifting taper
Internal source-concept index: NCEES 2023 Q61.

### Variation A
A work zone shifts a 12-ft lane laterally at 55 mph. For this project L=WS for merging taper and shifting taper is 1/2 L. Find shifting length.

- A. 220 ft
- B. 275 ft
- C. 330 ft
- D. 660 ft
**Answer: C.** Full L=12×55=660; shift=330 ft.

### Variation B
Shifting taper must be 300 ft at 50 mph and is one-half WS. Required shift width?

- A. 6 ft
- B. 10 ft
- C. 12 ft
- D. 15 ft
**Answer: C.** 300=0.5×W×50, hence W=12 ft.

### Variation C
A shift of 10 ft at 45 mph requires half of WS. Available taper lengths are 200, 225, 250, 300 ft; shortest adequate?

- A. 200 ft
- B. 225 ft
- C. 250 ft
- D. 300 ft
**Answer: B.** L=0.5×10×45=225 ft.

**Transfer skill:** Do not confuse merging and shifting taper fractions.

## TRAN-064 — Concrete cylinder compressive strength
Internal source-concept index: NCEES 2023 Q64.

### Variation A
A 6-in diameter concrete cylinder fails at 84,800 lb. Compressive stress most nearly?

- A. 1,500 psi
- B. 2,400 psi
- C. 3,000 psi
- D. 3,600 psi
**Answer: C.** Area=π×6²/4=28.274 in²; 84,800/28.274=2,999 psi.

### Variation B
Three 6-in cylinders fail at 80,000, 85,000 and 90,000 lb. Average nominal compressive strength?

- A. 2,500 psi
- B. 2,800 psi
- C. 3,000 psi
- D. 3,300 psi
**Answer: C.** Mean load=85,000 lb; divide by 28.274=3,006 psi.

### Variation C
A 4-in diameter cylinder must reach 4,000 psi. What corresponding failure load is most nearly?

- A. 25,100 lb
- B. 40,000 lb
- C. 50,300 lb
- D. 64,000 lb
**Answer: C.** P=fA=4,000×π(4²)/4=50,265 lb.

**Transfer skill:** Cylinder geometry and force units must remain consistent.
**Review note:** Independent engineering and reference review pending.

## TRAN-066 — Standard penetration test interpretation

For cohesionless soil, an SPT N-value is primarily used as which type of measure?

- A. Direct pore pressure
- B. Indirect relative-density indicator

- D. Direct permeability
**Answer: B.** SPT blow count provides an indirect index requiring corrections/interpretation.

### Variation B
A field log has N-value from blows in second and third 6-in drives of 9 and 12. N is?

- A. 9
- B. 12
- C. 21
- D. 30
**Answer: C.** Conventional SPT N=9+12=21 blows/ft; first 6-in seating drive excluded.

### Variation C
Recorded 6-in drive blow counts are 5, 8 and 10. Report uncorrected SPT N.

- A. 13
- B. 15
- C. 18
- D. 23
**Answer: C.** N=8+10=18, not the sum of all three intervals.

## TRAN-075 — Manning conveyance and discharge time
Internal source-concept index: NCEES 2023 Q75.

### Variation A
A channel conveys 12 cfs continuously. How long to discharge 18 acre-ft, ignoring inflow and variable stage?

- A. 12.1 hr
- B. 16.5 hr
- C. 18.2 hr
- D. 24.0 hr
**Answer: C.** Volume=18×43560=784080 ft³; time=784080/12/3600=18.15 hr.

### Variation B
A rectangular channel is 5 ft wide, water depth 2 ft, n=.025, slope .001. Using Q=(1.49/n)AR^(2/3)S^0.5, Q most nearly?

- A. 12 cfs
- B. 17 cfs
- C. 20 cfs
- D. 24 cfs
**Answer: C.** A=10; P=5+4=9; R=10/9; Q≈20.2 cfs.

**Transfer skill:** Manning determines capacity; volume divided by discharge determines time.

## TRAN-078 — Darcy-Weisbach pipe flow

A full 2-ft pipe has L=1000 ft, f=0.02, head loss 10 ft. Using hf=f(L/D)V²/(2g), g=32.2 ft/s², find velocity.

For fixed f,L,D, doubling velocity increases Darcy friction head loss by factor?

## TRAN-079 — Bernoulli pressure head
Internal source-concept index: NCEES 2023 Q79.

### Variation A
A horizontal lossless pipe contracts; flow speed rises from 4 to 8 ft/s. Pressure head change p2/γ−p1/γ is most nearly?

- A. −0.75 ft
- B. 0 ft
- C. +0.75 ft
- D. +1.5 ft
**Answer: A.** Δ(p/γ)=(V1²−V2²)/(2g)=(16−64)/64.4=−0.745 ft.

### Variation B
In lossless flow at equal velocities, point 2 is 5 ft higher than point 1. Pressure head at point 2 is?

- A. 5 ft higher
- B. unchanged
- C. 5 ft lower
- D. 10 ft lower
**Answer: C.** Bernoulli: pressure head drops 5 ft as elevation rises 5 ft.

### Variation C
In a level, lossless conduit, pressure head increases 3 ft. What happens to velocity head?

- A. Increases 3 ft
- B. Decreases 3 ft
- C. Unchanged
- D. Increases 6 ft
**Answer: B.** Total head conserved; a 3-ft rise in pressure head requires 3-ft loss of velocity head.

**Transfer skill:** State location/elevation and whether losses are ignored.
