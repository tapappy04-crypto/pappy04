## TRAN-041 — Crest curve sight distance
Internal source-concept index: NCEES 2023 Q41.

### Variation A
For a crest curve with a stipulated S>L equation L=2S−2800/A, S=500 ft and A=4 percentage points, find length.

- A. 200 ft
- B. 250 ft
- C. 300 ft
- D. 500 ft
**Answer: C.** L=1000−2800/4=300 ft; 500>300 confirms S>L.

### Variation B
A crest curve is 300 ft long with A=4 percentage points. Using S>L relation L=2S−2800/A, available sight distance is?

- A. 350 ft
- B. 400 ft
- C. 500 ft
- D. 600 ft
**Answer: C.** S=(L+2800/A)/2=(300+700)/2=500 ft, greater than L.

### Variation C
For S=425 ft and A=4%, the trial S≤L formula L=AS²/2800 produces 258 ft. Which is correct?

- A. 258 ft is valid
- B. Use S>L instead; L=150 ft
- C. Use L=425 ft
- D. Use L=700 ft
**Answer: B.** Trial 258<425 violates S≤L; alternate L=2×425−2800/4=150 ft, with S>L consistent.

**Transfer skill:** Always check formula domain; do not publish the two calculation-only items without the S>L alternate.
**Review note:** The denominator 2800 is stipulated to create a stand-alone equation-selection exercise.

## TRAN-047 — Ramp acceleration length
Internal source-concept index: NCEES 2023 Q47.

- A. 660 ft
- B. 968 ft
- C. 1,320 ft
- D. 1,650 ft
**Answer: B.** v1=44, v2=88 ft/s; L=(88²−44²)/(2×3)=968 ft.

### Variation B

- A. 1.3 ft/s²
- B. 2.2 ft/s²
- C. 3.2 ft/s²
- D. 4.2 ft/s²
**Answer: C.** v1=51.33, v2=80.67 ft/s; a=(v2²−v1²)/1200=3.23 ft/s².

### Variation C

- A. 55 mph
- B. 60 mph
- C. 65 mph
- D. 70 mph
**Answer: B.** v2=sqrt(66²+2×2.5×700)=88.63 ft/s=60.4 mph.

**Transfer skill:** These are intentionally simplified kinematic exercises, not substitutes for AASHTO acceleration-length design tables.
